Beginner's guide

Best deer cartridges for beginners

Almost any common centerfire deer cartridge will cleanly take a whitetail. What matters most is a bullet built for deer, a cartridge you can shoot well, and shots kept inside your own ethical range.

What to look for

Recoil you can handle. Flinching ruins more hunts than a lack of power. Lighter-recoiling rounds are easier to practice with and easier to shoot accurately.

Ammo you can find. Popular cartridges have more bullet choices, lower prices and stay on store shelves.

Your hunting distance. Brush and woods hunting rarely needs a flat-shooting magnum. Open country and long shots favor flatter, higher-BC loads.

Local rules. Some states set minimum calibers or limit deer hunting to straight-wall cartridges. Check your state's regulations before you buy.
