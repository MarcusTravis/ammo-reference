7.5x55 Swiss

Swiss service round with very accurate match-grade surplus.

Good for: WWI era, WWII era, Deer today, Elk today

A typical factory load is a 174 gr GP11 ball bullet at about 2,640 ft/s, with roughly 2,693 ft-lbs of energy at the muzzle.
