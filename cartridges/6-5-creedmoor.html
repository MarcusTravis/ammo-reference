6.5 Creedmoor

Designed for long-range target matches; adopted for deer, pronghorn and hogs. Elk with good bullets inside 400.

Good for: Deer/hog, Elk/moose

A typical factory load is a 143 gr ELD-X bullet at about 2,700 ft/s, with roughly 2,315 ft-lbs of energy at the muzzle.
