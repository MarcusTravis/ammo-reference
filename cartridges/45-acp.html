.45 ACP

John Browning's U.S. Army round for the 1911. Big, slow bullet for self-defense and bullseye target shooting.

Good for: Pistol, Self-defense, Target

A typical factory load is a 230 gr JHP bullet at about 850 ft/s, with roughly 369 ft-lbs of energy at the muzzle.
