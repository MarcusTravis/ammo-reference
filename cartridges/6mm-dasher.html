6mm Dasher

Benchrest, F-Class and PRS: tiny groups, very little recoil so you can spot your own hits.

Good for: PRS, F-Class, Benchrest

A typical factory load is a 105 gr Berger Hybrid bullet at about 2,950 ft/s, with roughly 2,029 ft-lbs of energy at the muzzle.
