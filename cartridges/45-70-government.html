Cartridge profile · Introduced 1873

.45-70 Government

Army rifle round that took bison. Today a lever-gun choice for bear, moose and elk up close.

Good for: Deer/hog, Elk/moose, Big/dangerous, Straight-wall

A typical factory load is a 300 gr hollow point bullet at about 1,880 ft/s, with roughly 2,354 ft-lbs of energy at the muzzle.
