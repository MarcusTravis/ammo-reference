22 ARC

Hornady's modern answer to the .223: 6mm ARC case necked to .22 for heavy, high-BC bullets. Coyotes and varmints far out, deer where .22s are legal, from an AR-15 or small bolt gun.

Good for: Varmint, Deer/hog

A typical factory load is a 88 gr ELD Match bullet at about 2,750 ft/s, with roughly 1,478 ft-lbs of energy at the muzzle.
