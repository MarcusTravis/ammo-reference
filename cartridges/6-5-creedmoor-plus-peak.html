6.5 Creedmoor +Peak

Federal's higher-pressure Peak Alloy case pushes a heavier bullet about 200 fps faster than standard 6.5 CM. This Gold Medal load is a match (target) bullet, so pick a +Peak hunting bullet for game.

Good for: Deer/hog

A typical factory load is a 153 gr Sierra MatchKing bullet at about 2,900 ft/s, with roughly 2,857 ft-lbs of energy at the muzzle.
