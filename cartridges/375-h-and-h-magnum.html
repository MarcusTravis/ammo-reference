.375 H&H Magnum

Designed for African dangerous game; the standard for Cape buffalo and big brown bear.

Good for: Big/dangerous

A typical factory load is a 300 gr soft point bullet at about 2,530 ft/s, with roughly 4,263 ft-lbs of energy at the muzzle.
