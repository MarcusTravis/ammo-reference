.300 Norma Magnum

U.S. SOCOM's choice for its Advanced Sniper Rifle. Heavy .30 bullets with huge reach and energy.

Good for: ELR, Military/LE

A typical factory load is a 230 gr Berger Hybrid OTM bullet at about 2,934 ft/s, with roughly 4,397 ft-lbs of energy at the muzzle.
