.22 LR Subsonic

Stays below the speed of sound, so it's very quiet, especially suppressed. Squirrels, rabbits and pests close in without spooking everything nearby.

Good for: Small game

A typical factory load is a 40 gr hollow point bullet at about 1,050 ft/s, with roughly 98 ft-lbs of energy at the muzzle.
