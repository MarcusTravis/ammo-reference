.500 S&W Magnum

The most powerful production revolver round. Big-game handgun hunting and defense against grizzly and brown bear.

Good for: Revolver, Hunting, Big game/bear

A typical factory load is a 350 gr XTP bullet at about 1,900 ft/s, with roughly 2,805 ft-lbs of energy at the muzzle.
