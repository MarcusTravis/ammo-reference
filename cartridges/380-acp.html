.380 ACP

Designed by John Browning for small pocket pistols. Mild recoil in tiny guns; meant for close-range self-defense.

Good for: Pistol, Self-defense

A typical factory load is a 90 gr JHP bullet at about 950 ft/s, with roughly 180 ft-lbs of energy at the muzzle.
