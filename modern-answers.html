Tips · cartridge design

Modern Answers to the Classics

Newer cartridges are usually shorter and fatter, with less of the bullet sitting inside the case. Here's each old classic next to the newer round built to replace or outdo it.

Shorter caseFits a shorter, lighter action and a shorter powder column, which tends to burn more consistently.
Less bullet insideLong, sleek bullets sit farther out of the case instead of eating the powder space, so they fit standard magazines.
Wider or straighter bodyWSMs and PRCs use fatter heads. Creedmoors keep the .308 head size but use less body taper and a sharper shoulder.
No beltNewer magnums headspace on the shoulder instead of the old belt, which is easier on brass.
