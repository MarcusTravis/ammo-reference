How these are figured

Drop and wind drift use a 100-yard zero, a scope 1.5" over the bore, sea-level standard air, a G1 drag model and a 10 mph full-value (90°) crosswind. MOA is 1.047" per 100 yards. Supersonic range is where the bullet falls below about 1,125 fps; past that point, accuracy often falls apart as it goes transonic.

The disciplines

  • PRS: Precision Rifle Series. Timed shots from awkward positions at steel targets out to about 1,200 yd.
  • F-Class: prone, scoped shooting at paper to 1,000 yd. Open class allows any caliber; F-TR is .308/.223 with a bipod.
  • Benchrest: smallest groups or best score from a rest, usually 100–300 yd.
  • ELR: extreme long range, a mile and beyond.

Keep in mind

  • Velocities, BCs, barrel life and twist are typical figures from my own knowledge, not looked up. Real long-range data should use your chronographed speed and G7 BC in a ballistic app.
  • Barrel life is the accurate life for match shooting; a hunting rifle shot less often lasts much longer in years.
  • Cost: $ = cheapest common match ammo, $$$$$ = several dollars or more per round.
