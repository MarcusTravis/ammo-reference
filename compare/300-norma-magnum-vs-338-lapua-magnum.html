.300 Norma Magnum vs .338 Lapua Magnum

Typical factory loads side by side: speed, energy, BC, twist, range and what each one is built for.

The short answer

With typical loads, the .300 Norma Magnum is about 100 ft/s faster at the muzzle (2,800 vs 2,700 ft/s).

The .338 Lapua Magnum carries about 21% more energy at the muzzle (4,856 vs 4,004 ft-lbs), which usually also means more recoil.

Which one fits you?

.300 Norma Magnum: U.S. SOCOM's choice for its Advanced Sniper Rifle. Heavy .30 bullets with huge reach and energy.

.338 Lapua Magnum: Long-range sniper round designed to reach 1,500 m with full power. Also a heavy-game hunting round.

For most hunters, the right pick is the one you can shoot accurately and comfortably, in a rifle that fits how you hunt. Good shot placement and a bullet built for your game matter more than small differences on paper.
