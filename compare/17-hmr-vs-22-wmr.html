Cartridge comparison

.17 HMR vs .22 WMR

Typical factory loads side by side: speed, energy, BC, twist, range and what each one is built for.

The short answer

With typical loads, the .17 HMR is about 640 ft/s faster at the muzzle (2,550 vs 1,910 ft/s).

The .22 WMR carries about 32% more energy at the muzzle (324 vs 245 ft-lbs), which usually also means more recoil.

On the same size target, the .17 HMR has the longer point-blank range (145 yd), so you can hold dead-on a bit farther without adjusting.

Which one fits you?

.17 HMR: .22 WMR necked to .17. Very flat and accurate for prairie dogs, squirrels and pests.

.22 WMR: Rimfire magnum for rabbits, groundhogs, foxes and coyotes close in.

For most hunters, the right pick is the one you can shoot accurately and comfortably, in a rifle that fits how you hunt. Good shot placement and a bullet built for your game matter more than small differences on paper.
