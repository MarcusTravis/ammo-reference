Cartridge comparison

.45-70 Government vs .450 Bushmaster

Typical factory loads side by side: speed, energy, BC, twist, range and what each one is built for.

The short answer

With typical loads, the .450 Bushmaster is about 320 ft/s faster at the muzzle (1,880 vs 2,200 ft/s).

The .450 Bushmaster carries about 14% more energy at the muzzle (2,687 vs 2,354 ft-lbs), which usually also means more recoil.

On the same size target, the .450 Bushmaster has the longer point-blank range (200 yd), so you can hold dead-on a bit farther without adjusting.

Which one fits you?

.45-70 Government: Army rifle round that took bison. Today a lever-gun choice for bear, moose and elk up close.

.450 Bushmaster: Big-bore AR round for deer, hogs and black bear; legal in most straight-wall states.

For most hunters, the right pick is the one you can shoot accurately and comfortably, in a rifle that fits how you hunt. Good shot placement and a bullet built for your game matter more than small differences on paper.
