.375 H&H Magnum vs .375 Ruger

Typical factory loads side by side: speed, energy, BC, twist, range and what each one is built for.

The short answer

With typical loads, the .375 Ruger is about 130 ft/s faster at the muzzle (2,530 vs 2,660 ft/s).

The .375 Ruger carries about 11% more energy at the muzzle (4,713 vs 4,263 ft-lbs), which usually also means more recoil.

On the same size target, the .375 Ruger has the longer point-blank range (255 yd), so you can hold dead-on a bit farther without adjusting.

Which one fits you?

.375 H&H Magnum: Designed for African dangerous game; the standard for Cape buffalo and big brown bear.

.375 Ruger: Modern beltless .375 that beats the H&H's speed in a standard-length action.

For most hunters, the right pick is the one you can shoot accurately and comfortably, in a rifle that fits how you hunt. Good shot placement and a bullet built for your game matter more than small differences on paper.
