10mm Auto vs .44 Magnum

Typical factory loads side by side: speed, energy, BC, twist, range and what each one is built for.

The short answer

With typical loads, the .44 Magnum is about 100 ft/s faster at the muzzle (1,250 vs 1,350 ft/s).

The .44 Magnum carries about 56% more energy at the muzzle (971 vs 624 ft-lbs), which usually also means more recoil.

Which one fits you?

10mm Auto: Powerful semi-auto round for hog and deer hunting and woods carry against black bear.

.44 Magnum: Made famous as the powerful handgun of its day. Deer, hogs and black bear; backup against big bears.

For most hunters, the right pick is the one you can shoot accurately and comfortably, in a rifle that fits how you hunt. Good shot placement and a bullet built for your game matter more than small differences on paper.
