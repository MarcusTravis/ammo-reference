.300 Winchester Mag vs .338 Winchester Mag

Typical factory loads side by side: speed, energy, BC, twist, range and what each one is built for.

The short answer

With typical loads, the .300 Winchester Mag is about 180 ft/s faster at the muzzle (2,960 vs 2,780 ft/s).

The .338 Winchester Mag carries about 10% more energy at the muzzle (3,861 vs 3,502 ft-lbs), which usually also means more recoil.

On the same size target, the .300 Winchester Mag has the longer point-blank range (286 yd), so you can hold dead-on a bit farther without adjusting.

Which one fits you?

.300 Winchester Mag: Long-range elk and moose cartridge; also used on big bears and plains game.

.338 Winchester Mag: Built for elk, moose and brown bear where heavy bullets and deep penetration matter.

For most hunters, the right pick is the one you can shoot accurately and comfortably, in a rifle that fits how you hunt. Good shot placement and a bullet built for your game matter more than small differences on paper.
