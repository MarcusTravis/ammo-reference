Cartridge comparison

9mm Luger vs .40 S&W

Typical factory loads side by side: speed, energy, BC, twist, range and what each one is built for.

The short answer

With typical loads, the 9mm Luger is about 150 ft/s faster at the muzzle (1,150 vs 1,000 ft/s).

The .40 S&W carries about 10% more energy at the muzzle (400 vs 364 ft-lbs), which usually also means more recoil.

On the same size target, the 9mm Luger has the longer point-blank range (109 yd), so you can hold dead-on a bit farther without adjusting.

Which one fits you?

9mm Luger: Georg Luger's military round, now the most popular handgun cartridge. Self-defense, police and competition with manageable recoil.

.40 S&W: Shortened 10mm built for the FBI and police. More punch than 9mm in a standard-size pistol.

For most hunters, the right pick is the one you can shoot accurately and comfortably, in a rifle that fits how you hunt. Good shot placement and a bullet built for your game matter more than small differences on paper.
