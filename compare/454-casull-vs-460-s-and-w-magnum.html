.454 Casull vs .460 S&W Magnum

Typical factory loads side by side: speed, energy, BC, twist, range and what each one is built for.

The short answer

With typical loads, the .460 S&W Magnum is about 350 ft/s faster at the muzzle (1,650 vs 2,000 ft/s).

The .460 S&W Magnum carries about 47% more energy at the muzzle (2,664 vs 1,813 ft-lbs), which usually also means more recoil.

On the same size target, the .460 S&W Magnum has the longer point-blank range (174 yd), so you can hold dead-on a bit farther without adjusting.

Which one fits you?

.454 Casull: Long, high-pressure .45 Colt for big-game handgun hunting and bear defense. Heavy recoil.

.460 S&W Magnum: The fastest production revolver round. Built for long-range handgun hunting of elk, moose and big bears.

For most hunters, the right pick is the one you can shoot accurately and comfortably, in a rifle that fits how you hunt. Good shot placement and a bullet built for your game matter more than small differences on paper.
