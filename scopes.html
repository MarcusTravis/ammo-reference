How the ratings work

  • Popularity: 2026 best-seller lists (Amazon, "most bought" roundups), editor picks (Outdoor Life, GunsAmerica) and what top precision shooters use (PrecisionRifleBlog).
  • Glass: owner and reviewer reputation for clarity, edge sharpness and low-light brightness.
  • Tracking & toughness: reputation for turrets moving exactly as marked, returning to zero and holding up to recoil and drops.

Quick terms

  • FFP vs. SFP: a first-focal-plane reticle grows with magnification, so holdovers are right at every power (best for long range). Second focal plane stays the same size (simpler for hunting).
  • Zero stop: the elevation turret stops at your zero, so you can always dial back fast.
  • LPVO: low-power variable optic (1-6x, 1-8x) for ARs and close, fast shots.
  • Tube size mostly decides how much elevation you can dial and which rings you need.
