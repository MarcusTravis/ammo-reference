How MPBR is figured

From a rifle with a scope 1.5" over the bore, sea-level air and a G1 drag model. Every round gets a 2" small-game zone (squirrel head or body); the .17s and .22 WMR also get a 4" varmint zone. Sight-in height is given at 50 yards, the usual rimfire zero distance.

Keep in mind

  • Rimfire accuracy varies a lot by ammo brand; try several in your rifle.
  • .17 bullets are light and blow around in wind more than their speed suggests.
  • Velocities and BCs are typical rifle figures from my own knowledge, not looked up. Pistols run slower.
