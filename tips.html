Cartridge Tips & Tricks

The stuff long-time shooters picked up over years at the range and the gun counter, and that beginners usually learn the hard way. Tips marked Must know prevent accidents or wasted hunts. Should know tips are worth learning early.

General guidance, not load data. Always go by the markings on your barrel and the maker's manual, and check your state's hunting rules for caliber and bullet requirements.
