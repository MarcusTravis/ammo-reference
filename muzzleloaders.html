How MPBR is figured

Traditional sidelocks, flintlocks and conical setups assume open iron sights about 0.6" over the bore. The inline sabot row assumes a scope 1.5" over the bore. Zones are 2" for small game, 4" for turkey and small predators, 6" for deer and up, with sight-in height at 50 yards. Round balls lose speed quickly, so point-blank range is short.

Powder and twist

  • Granulation: Fg is coarse (big bores), FFg for .45 and up, FFFg for small bores and pan priming in flintlocks use FFFFg.
  • Slow twists (1:48 and slower) suit patched round balls; fast twists (1:28) suit conicals and sabots.
  • Never use smokeless powder unless the muzzleloader is specifically built and marked for it.

Keep in mind

Charges, velocities and BCs are typical figures from my own knowledge, not looked up; muzzleloader speed varies a lot with powder brand, patch and barrel length. Follow your rifle maker's maximum charge and your state's muzzleloader season rules.
