⚠

Same bullet does NOT mean same ammo

Rounds that share a bullet diameter are not interchangeable. Firing the wrong cartridge can destroy your gun and seriously injure or kill you or anyone near you.

Only shoot the exact cartridge stamped on your barrel.

Bullet diameter is the measured size of the bullet itself. Same-size bullets can still differ in weight, length and construction, so check your twist rate and published load data when handloading.
