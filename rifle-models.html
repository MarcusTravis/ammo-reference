How the ratings work

  • Popularity reflects 2026 sales reports (GunBroker, Guns.com and dealer rankings) plus how common the rifle is at ranges and in hunting camps.
  • Accuracy is the general owner and reviewer reputation for 3-shot groups at 100 yd (50 yd for rimfires) with ammo the rifle likes. 1 MOA is about 1" at 100 yd.
  • Guarantee is a factory accuracy promise where one exists.

Prices

Ranges are typical new street prices across a model's common versions, not MSRP. Special editions, magnum calibers and premium stocks cost more; sales and used rifles cost less. Check current prices before you buy.

Sources and limits

Popularity draws on GunBroker's 2026 top sellers (CVA Scout, Ruger 10/22, Ruger American), Guns.com's October 2026 report (Radical RF-15) and Field & Stream's 2026 best rifles (Tikka T3x Lite). Prices, specs and accuracy reputations are from my own knowledge, not looked up model by model.
