Every chart checked against maker data

November 12, 2026

All 298 rows on the 17 charts were matched to a real factory load or product and checked against the maker's published numbers (or SAAMI and spec sheets where there is no single maker load). Wrong values were fixed everywhere they appear, including cartridge profiles, comparisons and the deer guide, and energy and point-blank ranges were recalculated. 174 rows changed, many of them only by a recalculated point-blank range or an updated price.

  • Hunting rifle cartridges 33 rows · 15 updated
    Corrected 22 ARC, 6mm ARC and .25 Creedmoor speeds to Hornady's catalog; fixed BCs on .223, .270, 6mm Rem and .338 Win Mag; relabeled .25 PRC as a handload-only wildcat.
  • Precision rifle 13 rows · 4 updated
    .300 Norma Magnum now matches Berger's factory load (2,934 ft/s); .375 CheyTac and .416 Barrett BCs corrected, with drop, wind and supersonic range recomputed.
  • Wildcat cartridges 14 rows · 5 updated
    .30-30 Ackley BC lowered to match real 150 gr flat-nose bullets; four out-of-date point-blank ranges recomputed.
  • Military surplus 18 rows · 6 updated
    7.5x55 Swiss GP 11 speed and 7.62x54R BC corrected; four point-blank ranges recomputed.
  • AR calibers 20 rows · 5 updated
    22 ARC, 6mm ARC, 6.8 SPC, .50 Beowulf and .338 Federal now match their makers' published loads.
  • Rimfire 7 rows · 2 updated
    .22 LR and .22 WMR point-blank ranges recomputed.
  • Big bore 13 rows · 9 updated
    Loads confirmed; point-blank ranges recomputed so .375 H&H matches the Hunting chart.
  • Handgun cartridges 17 rows · 13 updated
    5.7x28 (1,738 ft/s, FN), .38 Special (755 ft/s), .454 Casull and .460 S&W loads corrected; small point-blank fixes elsewhere.
  • Shotshells & slugs 17 rows · 6 updated
    .410 load is the 3" shell; 20 ga sabot BC from Hornady; CCI rimfire and revolver shotshells updated to CCI's 2026 catalog.
  • Rifle models 22 rows · 4 updated
    Browning X-Bolt 2 and CZ 457 trigger specs, Christensen barrel wording and the CVA Scout sales claim corrected.
  • Rifle scopes 25 rows · 14 updated
    Bushnell R7 is the 4-20x50 FFP; Burris Fullfield E1 and Nightforce SHV 4-14x56 replace discontinued models; weights fixed to maker specs.
  • Thermal scopes 20 rows · 20 updated
    Every row updated: sensor resolution fixed on six models, discontinued models renamed to current ones, and prices, weights and battery life set from maker pages.
  • Thermal monoculars 20 rows · 20 updated
    Prices, weights, battery life and detection ranges filled in or corrected from maker pages; Telos XP50 now shows in the Rangefinder filter.
  • Laser rangefinders 17 rows · 11 updated
    Prices, deer and reflective ranges and weights updated from maker pages; KILO3K and Midas 1 Mile now sit under $300.
  • Hunting binoculars 21 rows · 21 updated
    Missing field-of-view and weight figures filled in; ZULU4, Kite APC, Toric and Viper HD prices corrected; Razor HD marked discontinued.
  • Muzzleloaders 12 rows · 10 updated
    Speeds checked against GOEX and Hodgdon load data; .50 sabot, .54 conical, .62 and .72 loads corrected; point-blank ranges recomputed.
  • Airguns 9 rows · 9 updated
    .20 pellet set to a real 11.42 gr pellet, .357 shown as JSB's 81 gr domed pellet, and point-blank ranges recomputed.

Rows I could not confirm against a published source are listed per chart in the changelog.

Data consistency fixes

November 12, 2026

A new automatic check compares every chart with the cartridge profiles, comparisons and deer guide. It found a handful of loads shown with different numbers in different places (.308 Win, .243 Win, 7mm-08, 6mm GT, .22 LR Subsonic, 6mm Dasher, .284 Shehane). Each was set to the maker's published figure, not copied from another chart.
